# MixEhr — every style and its controls

375 styles in 16 families. Each family is one plugin ("MixEhr <Family>") with a **Style** dropdown; the styles are
listed here in dropdown order. In Arena's panel every style has a heading row with its name, and its own controls sit under it.

Every family also has **Style**, **Curve** and **Ends At** at the top (and **Angle** / **File** where its styles use them),
and **Debug Log** at the bottom. How the controls behave: see the [How-to](README.md#f-what-the-controls-do).

- A slider at **0.5**, a switch **off**, a colour with alpha **0** and **Angle 0** = the style's own default look.
- *(colour)* = a colour picker; its alpha is how much of your colour replaces the style's own.
- *(switch)* = on / off.
- **Angle** = the shared Angle control at the top, in degrees counter-clockwise from the style's own direction.
- **File** = the shared File control at the top (PNG / JPG, or a DXV .mov for a stinger).
